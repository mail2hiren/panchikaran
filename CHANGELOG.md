# Changelog

## v2.0.0 — 2026-11-09
Added what a guru would expect before and around the teaching.
- **New chapter 1, "Before we begin: the seeker"**: viveka, vairāgya, the six treasures, mumukṣutva; approaching the guru (Gītā 4.34); the four anubandhas; śravaṇa–manana–nididhyāsana. With a memory table that can be hidden and self-tested.
- **New chapter "Sat · Chit · Ānanda"** (before the witness): existence, awareness, fullness; asat–jaḍa–duḥkha; asti–bhāti–priya with nāma–rūpa; the bliss sheath as a reflection; taṭastha and svarūpa lakṣaṇa.
- **New last chapter "Om Tat Sat and the great sayings"**: Gītā 17.23–28, the four mahāvākyas, tat tvam asi by bhāga-tyāga, adhyāropa–apavāda.
- **A story for 10 chapters**, as a second animated, narrated tab: Nachiketa, Uddālaka and Śvetaketu, the gods entering the body (Aitareya), the chariot (Kaṭha), the senses' quarrel (Praśna), Bhṛgu, Indra and Virochana, the yaksha (Kena), the tenth man, the pot-space and the wave.
- **Deities explained**: adhyātma–adhibhūta–adhidaiva, the Trimūrti and the guṇas, the inner-instrument deities.
- **Source verses** (Devanagari, transliteration, meaning) on 8 chapters.
- Extra notes: māyā's āvaraṇa and vikṣepa, the lamp on the stage, crystal and red flower, neti neti.
- Quiz and flashcards now include the qualifications and asti–bhāti–priya.
- Narration for the new chapters and stories plays with the device voice until recorded audio is generated (`tools/make_audio.py`).

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-06
First visual version: 11 animated chapters with the seated figure, zoomable pictures, recorded Indian male narration in 4 languages, tables, flashcards, quiz, glossary.
